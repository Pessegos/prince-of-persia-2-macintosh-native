## Recovered Decisions

CurrentCell4:35a6 runs **before** the ALIVE and selected-source checks.
Nonnegative native ALIVE (actor28) or source118 then returns. HP (actor30)
does not replace ALIVE: an ALIVE-negative/HP-zero fixture still reaches the
contact branch. Otherwise use the first tile4 among current, FrontCell,
GetCellsInFront(1). This neighbor order is the reverse of
[GateBarrToStandUp](temple-gate-standing.md). The first gate wins even if
its later overlap/admission test fails; no search for a second blocking gate.

The resolved cached room9/level8 case exits. Two overlap-bank words for the
resolved column must have bitwise intersection **equal to255**, not just
contain the low eight bits. Only then is native4:5b0e consulted.

When blocked, ordinary pose other than109, a nonzero **whole foreground
word**, and armed word other than1 choose the soft branch. If selected
source is already50, neither sequence selection nor distance calculation
repeats. Otherwise JumpSeq50 runs before distance4:5eae. Distance below25
corrects X with AddCharX(distance-25);25 or greater leaves X unchanged.
Signed word wrapping is preserved. If SEQS50 lookup fails, the previous
cursor/active/source survive but the distance correction still runs.
There is no AnimChar call in this helper, so source50 is not a newly drawn
pose until a separately scheduled animation consumes it.

Other blocked cases route to the harder branch. Environment3, nonzero low
foreground byte and unarmed state dispatch to6:2ac8. Otherwise4:5a70 sets
the contact mark at A5-4f9e (except kind1) and calls4:6202 for ejection.
This probe does not execute either terminal response: **the mark is not
proof of damage, HP zero, ALIVE change or registered death**. In environment2
the cave-specific terminal branch is not selected. A high-byte-only
foreground word can choose soft contact even though admission sees low
position zero; the two tests deliberately use different masks.
