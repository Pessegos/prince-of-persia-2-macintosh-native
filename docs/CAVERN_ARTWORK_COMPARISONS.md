# Cavern Artwork Comparisons

Original Macintosh scenery and the artwork retouches in the port.

These comparisons include column shadows, wall and floor joins, floor edges around column bases and dart traps, and doorway edges across levels 3–5.

Open a room below to compare its artwork. Each GIF alternates between Macintosh and Port with identical framing. The Port images include the overhauled lava.

The scenery is shown without characters, with entrance doors closed. In level 5, room 7, the stone gate is open to reveal the wall behind it.
